# CliffExtract

CliffExtract is a decentralized social impact platform that transforms community funding through innovative milestone-based smart contracts and predictive impact modeling. Unlike traditional crowdfunding platforms, it employs a unique "cliff vesting" mechanism where funds are released in predetermined tranches only when measurable impact milestones are achieved and verified through a hybrid oracle system. The platform combines IoT sensors, community validators, and AI-powered outcome prediction algorithms to ensure authentic impact verification.

The technical architecture features extractable smart contracts that can reclaim and redistribute unused or ineffective funding based on predetermined performance thresholds, ensuring maximum capital efficiency for social good. Each project deployment creates a unique impact NFT that evolves visually and functionally as milestones are reached, serving as both a progress tracker and tradeable proof-of-impact certificate. The platform implements cross-chain interoperability, allowing impact projects to access funding from multiple blockchain ecosystems while maintaining a unified impact measurement standard.

CliffExtract enables dynamic funding pools that automatically adjust based on real-time impact metrics, allowing successful projects to unlock additional community-driven bonus funding while underperforming initiatives face gradual fund extraction back to the community treasury. Community members can stake governance tokens to validate impact claims and earn rewards for accurate predictions about project success rates. Real-world applications include disaster relief coordination with verified aid distribution, educational initiatives with measurable learning outcomes, and environmental projects verified through satellite conservation metrics.
